Who we are

Engineering and construction built for the long run

JR Construcción & Remodelación is a Costa Rican construction and remodeling company that brings engineering, architecture and on-site execution together under one team. We work on residential, commercial and hospitality projects nationwide, with quality-control processes, digital schedules and constant communication with the client.

Our mission is to deliver work that combines structural soundness, energy efficiency and a finish that lasts. Our vision is to be Costa Rica's go-to builder for projects that demand technical precision and a flawless service experience.
